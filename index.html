1. GROUND 1 (Bona Fide Recipient): The recipient has complied with Section 16(2) by possessing genuine tax invoices, proof of physical receipt of goods/services, and verifiable banking remittances.

2. GROUND 2 (High Court Precedent): As held by Madras High Court in D.Y. Beathel Enterprises (2021) and Calcutta High Court in Suncraft Energy (2023), coercive recovery against a bona fide recipient without first exhausting statutory recovery against the defaulting supplier is legally impermissible.

3. GROUND 3 (Section 170 CGST Act): Rounding differences within ±₹1.00 are statutorily protected.

4. GROUND 4 (Form GSTR-1A Intimation): Defaulting vendors have been served intra-month amendment intimations under CBIC Notification No. 12/2024-CT.
