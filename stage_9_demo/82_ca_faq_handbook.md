## 1. Statutory Provisions & Engineering Mappings

### Q1: What is the exact statutory basis for Section 16(2)(aa) 100% Matching?
* **Statutory Law:** Section 16(2)(aa) of the CGST Act, 2017 (effective Jan 1, 2022 via Finance Act, 2021) mandates that Input Tax Credit can **only** be availed if the details of the invoice or debit note have been furnished by the supplier in Form GSTR-1 and communicated to the recipient in Form GSTR-2B.
* **Engineering Implementation:** ReconcileGST strictly isolates any invoice present in the ERP Purchase Register but absent in GSTR-2B with the status `MISSING_IN_GSTR2B`. It locks the eligible ITC calculation to ₹0.00 for these vouchers, preventing illegal claims and subsequent DRC-01C electronic notices.

---

### Q2: How does Section 170 Rounding Tolerance work mathematically?
* **Statutory Law:** Section 170 of the CGST Act, 2017 mandates that the amount of tax, interest, penalty, fine, or any other sum payable, and the amount of refund or any other sum due, shall be rounded off to the nearest rupee.
* **Engineering Implementation:** When matching invoice records, if the Supplier GSTIN, normalized Invoice Number, and Date match, and the tax difference satisfies $|\Delta\text{Tax}| \le 100\text{ Paise}$ ($\pm ₹1.00$), the invoice is classified as `MATCHED` under `SECTION_170_ROUNDING_PASS_2`.

---

### Q3: What are the dual trigger thresholds for Rule 88D (Form GST DRC-01C)?
* **Statutory Law:** Under Rule 88D of the CGST Rules, 2017 (inserted via Notification No. 38/2023-CT), an automated Part A intimation in Form GST DRC-01C is generated by the GSTN portal when:
  $$\Delta\text{ITC} > 20.0\% \quad \text{AND} \quad \Delta\text{ITC} > ₹25,00,000\text{ (Rupees Twenty-Five Lakhs)}$$
* **Engineering Implementation:** `evaluateRule88DThreat()` computes both conditions simultaneously using integer Paise math:
  - If both conditions are exceeded, it fires a `CRITICAL` risk alert, activates a 7-day statutory clock, and warns against Rule 59(6)(e) GSTR-1 billing lockouts and Rule 142B bank attachments.
  - If only one condition is exceeded, it sets risk to `MEDIUM`.

---

### Q4: How does the system handle Rule 37A (180-Day Supplier Non-Filing Reversals)?
* **Statutory Law:** Under Rule 37A of the CGST Rules, 2017, if a supplier reports an invoice in Form GSTR-1 but fails to file Form GSTR-3B for that tax period by the 30th of November following the financial year, the recipient must reverse the claimed ITC along with interest under Section 50.
* **Engineering Implementation:** Our `Rule 37A Watchdog` tracks the supplier's GSTR-3B filing flag from the GSTR-2B payload and invoice age:
  - Vouchers pending $>180\text{ days}$ without GSTR-3B filing are bucketed into `CRITICAL_180_DAYS_HOLD` and flagged for vendor payment withholding.

---

### Q5: What is Form GSTR-1A Delta Generation for supplier dispute recovery?
* **Statutory Law:** Notified under CBIC Notification No. 12/2024-CT, Form GSTR-1A allows suppliers to amend outward supplies intra-month before filing GSTR-3B.
* **Engineering Implementation:** When a discrepancy is detected (e.g. rate mismatch, invoice number typo, or missing invoice), ReconcileGST generates a structured `GSTR-1A Delta JSON Payload`. This payload is automatically embedded into the 1-Click WhatsApp payment-hold intimation, allowing the supplier to upload and fix the return within minutes.

---

### Q6: How does the DRC-01C Legal Defense annexure leverage judicial precedents?
* **Statutory Precedents:**
  1. **Madras High Court in *D.Y. Beathel Enterprises (2021)*:** Held that when the recipient has paid tax to the supplier, the tax department cannot initiate coercive recovery against the recipient without first investigating and exhausting proceedings against the defaulting seller.
  2. **Calcutta High Court in *Suncraft Energy (2023)* (affirmed by Supreme Court):** Affirmed that non-reflection in GSTR-2A/2B cannot be the sole basis to deny ITC to a bona fide buyer who satisfies Section 16(2).
* **Engineering Implementation:** When Rule 88D is triggered, ReconcileGST auto-drafts a complete Form GST DRC-01C Part B legal reply citing both High Court authorities, Section 170 rounding protection, and Section 16(2) compliance records.
